Guardrails: safe by design

Built for kids first. Here is what is, and is not, in the app.

🙈

No real names or emails for kids

Heroes get a made-up name from a list. We never ask for real names, emails, birthdays, photos, home addresses or location.

🔐

Picture passwords

Kids sign in with a hero code and a four-picture password. Their friend code is separate, so sharing it never shares their sign-in.

💬

No open chat

Kids cannot write profiles or send private messages. In-game chat is short, filtered for bad words, links and phone numbers, only works for kids in a teacher's class, and pauses after blocked messages.

🚩

Report and remove

Drawings can be reported, and a reported drawing disappears immediately for review.

🚫

No ads, no trackers, nothing sold

The app is free and run for the school. Fonts load from the app itself, and the one video uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced player.

🔗

No outside links

Kids stay inside the app. There are no links out to other websites.

👀

Grown-ups approve

Teachers and the Sensei approve kindness points and teacher accounts. Parents link to their child with a one-time code.

🗑️

Delete on request

A parent or the school can ask for a hero and all their data to be deleted at any time. Sign-in IP addresses are erased after 30 days.

😌

Gentle design

Nothing flashes more than three times a second, and every game has an easy way out.
