# Credits

## Rhythm Tap songs

Every song in Rhythm Tap is a melody written out as notes in `src/lib/songs.ts` and played by the app's own
synthesizer (`src/lib/synth.ts`). No recordings are used, so no audio files are bundled and nobody's recording
rights are involved. The melodies of the older tunes are public domain (the composers died long ago, or the tunes
are traditional and published before 1930). The note-by-note arrangements, the bass lines and the beat maps were
written for this app. The "Composed for HAHN Heroes" tracks are original and dedicated to the public domain
(CC0 1.0). No song needs attribution; the song picker still shows the composer or source for each one.

Rule for adding songs: only public domain, CC0, or a license that clearly allows redistribution in an app.
No pop songs and no unclear sources. If a CC-BY track is ever used, add its credit here and in the app.
To use real recordings later, add the audio file, its source link and license to this table first.
