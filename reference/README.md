## Fixes to apply whenever these are used

- **School look:** at school, every squad kid wears the standard uniform (navy and violet Nexus jacket with the star emblem, plain tee, dark cargo pants, sneakers). This includes Kacee.
- **Cardinal logo:** use only `brand/davison-cardinal-logo.png`. The cardinal heads on Kacee's sheets are not approved.
- **Luna's shirt:** no slogan. Use a plain tee or a small crescent-moon graphic.
- **Spelling:** "Different Worlds, Brighter Together" (Anayah's sheets misspell it). His name is "Mr. Chopstick," and the team is "H.A.H.N. Heroes" (his sheet says "HAMR").
- **Nexus forms:** Luna and Isabella don't have Nexus forms yet. Make them before an episode needs them.
