I'm making Episode 1 of a manga for a kids' learning app. I've uploaded the reference art. Make 12 images, ONE AT A TIME, in the order below. After each image, stop and wait for me to say "next". Follow the style rules for every image.

STYLE RULES (apply to all 12)
- H.A.H.N. Heroes style: bright modern anime, clean bold line art, cel shading with glowing light, deep violet and navy base with gold, cyan and pink accents. Full-color manga pages.
- World: the Hidden Nexus, a secret glowing realm hidden inside Hahn Intermediate School. Its symbol is the four-point violet star.
- Match every face and hairstyle exactly to my reference sheets:
  - Ana: shaggy dark-blonde hair, freckles, blue eyes.
  - Kacee: very long dark-red hair, brown eyes.
  - Anayah: long wavy strawberry-blonde hair with pink streaks, brown eyes.
  - Luna: long brown hair fading to blue and violet at the ends, blue-gray eyes.
  - Isabella: long layered brown hair with golden highlights, brown eyes.
  - The Sensei: tall, hood always up, face hidden in shadow except a stubbled jaw and a kind smile. NEVER show his full face. Black robes with gold trim and violet sashes, triangle emblem on his chest.
  - Mrs. Collins, the principal: shoulder-length curly blonde hair with bangs, warm smile. Her First Keeper form matches my reference.
- All five girls wear the standard school uniform from my uniform reference: navy and violet zip jacket with the violet star emblem, plain black or white tee, dark cargo pants, black and white sneakers. No words or slogans on any clothing.
- The girls are 10 and 11 years old, with friendly, school-appropriate proportions and modest outfits.
- NEVER put speech bubbles, captions, text, letters or numbers in any image. Speech bubbles get added later, so wherever someone talks, leave clear open space (sky, wall or plain background) near that character's head, about a fifth of the panel, with no faces or important details in it.
- The only logos allowed are the violet Nexus star and, if a school cardinal appears, the Davison "D" cardinal I uploaded.
