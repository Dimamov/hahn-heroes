# Episode 1: The Hidden Nexus — ChatGPT art prompts

Twelve images that replace the current Episode 1 art one for one. Each matches a panel in `content/story.json`, so the new files drop straight in.

## How to run it

1. Start a fresh ChatGPT chat.
2. Upload these references from `reference/` (download them from GitHub first; ChatGPT needs the images attached in the chat, it can't see them in the repo):
   - `characters/ana-sheet.png`, `kacee-sheet.png`, `anayah-sheet.png`, `luna-sheet.png`, `isabella-sheet.png`
   - `characters/squad-school-uniform.png`
   - `characters/sensei.png`
   - `characters/first-keeper-mrs-collins.png`
   - `brand/davison-cardinal-logo.png`
3. Paste the style lock below once.
4. Paste each page prompt in order. Save each result as a PNG with the file name shown.
5. Put the PNGs in `11-story/ep01/` of the art folder and run the asset script, or send them to Claude to wire in.

## Style lock (paste once, first)

```text
For every image in this chat, use the H.A.H.N. Heroes style: bright modern anime, clean bold line art, cel shading with glowing light, deep violet and navy base with gold, cyan and pink accents. Full-color manga pages.

The world: the Hidden Nexus, a secret glowing realm hidden inside Hahn Intermediate School. Its symbol is the four-point violet star.

The cast. Match every face and hairstyle exactly to the reference sheets I uploaded:
- Ana: shaggy dark-blonde hair, freckles, blue eyes.
- Kacee: very long dark-red hair, brown eyes.
- Anayah: long wavy strawberry-blonde hair with pink streaks, brown eyes.
- Luna: long brown hair fading to blue and violet at the ends, blue-gray eyes.
- Isabella: long layered brown hair with golden highlights, brown eyes.
- The Sensei: tall, hood always up, face hidden in shadow except a stubbled jaw and a kind smile, black robes with gold trim and violet sashes, a triangle emblem on his chest.
- Mrs. Collins, the principal: shoulder-length curly blonde hair with bangs, warm smile. Her First Keeper form is in the reference I uploaded.

All five girls wear the standard school uniform from the uniform reference: navy and violet zip jacket with the violet star emblem, plain black or white tee, dark cargo pants, black and white sneakers. No slogans or words on any shirt.

The girls are 10 and 11 years old, with friendly, school-appropriate proportions and modest outfits.

Never put speech bubbles, captions, text, letters or numbers in any image. The app adds the words.

The only logos allowed are the violet Nexus star and, if a school cardinal appears, the Davison "D" cardinal I uploaded. No other team logos.
```
